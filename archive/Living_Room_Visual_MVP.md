Objective: create a reusable interior-rendering foundation, delivered first through a polished living-room experience that matches the supplied contemporary references.
In scope:
Reusable interior scene foundation
Room shell: floor, walls, ceiling, doors, windows.
Correct real-world units, placement, collision/grounding rules.
Camera and scene bounds that work for any future room type.

Reusable feature-wall system
Vertical fluted/slatted wood panels.
Plain painted panels, marble panels, decorative mouldings.
Display niches, integrated shelves, and warm LED backlighting.
Configurable dimensions, offsets, colours, and materials.

Reusable millwork system
Floating media consoles.
Base cabinets, drawers, shelves, tall storage, glass display cabinets.
Parameters for door style, handle style, shelf spacing, plinth/floating height, and lighting.
Designed to later serve wardrobes and kitchens.

Reusable material library
Walnut, natural oak, off-white paint, cream fabric, charcoal metal, brass, marble, clear/reeded glass.
Texture maps, sensible real-world texture scale, roughness/metalness, normal maps, and grain direction.
Material assignment controls at object or surface level.

Reusable lighting system
Recessed ceiling lights.
Linear/cove LEDs.
Niche/display lights.
Window daylight and soft ambient light.
Warm evening and neutral daytime presets.

Living-room MVP composition
Fluted TV feature wall with mounted TV.
Floating media cabinet.
Lit glass display niche.
Sofa/seating, coffee table, rug, curtains, fan, plants, and restrained decor.
Marble/light stone floor and warm neutral wall palette.
Curated “Warm Contemporary” preset matching the supplied direction.

Presentation rendering
Locked, reference-style hero cameras: frontal elevation, wide room, and angled detail.
High-quality render preset with shadows, exposure, tone mapping, and output sizing.
Saved render result and comparison workflow.

UX
Choose a Living Room preset.
Edit materials, feature-wall design, cabinet arrangement, lighting, furnishings, and camera.
Render a client-ready hero image without needing technical scene knowledge.

Quality bar
No floating/intersecting objects.
Consistent material scale and wood-grain direction.
Warm lighting without washed-out whites.
Render QA for missing assets/materials and incorrect camera framing.
Unit/build/end-to-end verification for the complete flow.

Explicitly out of scope for this milestone:
Bedroom, kitchen, wardrobe, entry-room presets.
Full photorealistic ray-tracing engine.
Custom 3D asset modelling tools.
Manufacturing/cut-list changes unrelated to the living-room visual workflow.
Definition of done: a user can open the app, choose the Living Room Visual MVP preset, adjust its core design parameters, and export a polished hero render visually aligned with these references.
