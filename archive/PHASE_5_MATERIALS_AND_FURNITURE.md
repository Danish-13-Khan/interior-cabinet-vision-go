# Phase 5 — Materials and Furniture

**Status:** Complete

## Delivered

- Floor and active-wall material assignment using the shared project material library.
- Cabinet material slots remain editable through the right inspector.
- Searchable, filterable furniture and decor catalog with parametric placements.
- Persistent layer controls for walls, openings, and furniture/decor in both plan data and compiled 3D scene.

## Acceptance path

1. Open **Materials** and select finishes for the floor and active wall.
2. Open **Furniture**, search or filter the catalog, and place furniture or decor.
3. Select a cabinet and update its material slots in the inspector.
4. Open **Layers** and hide/show walls, openings, or furniture before switching to 3D.
