# Phase 8 — Advanced Studio

The Advanced Studio rail is the product foundation for assisted and multi-party workflows:

- A local, calibrated floor-plan draft fit that must be confirmed before authoring.
- Actionable layout suggestions that place real catalog items.
- A persistent floor stack, review notes, vendor comparison estimates, and a copyable client-review brief.

This is intentionally a local MVP. Production AI recognition, real-time multi-user collaboration, vendor inventory sync, and hosted client portal access require authenticated backend services and are not represented as completed integrations.
